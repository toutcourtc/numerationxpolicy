Privacy Policy of Numeration Primary

Introduction

Welcome to Numeration Primary. Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy describes the information we collect and how we use it. However, we assure you that our application does not collect any personal information and does not require a login to function.

Data Collection

Apart from the advertising ID (used for targeted advertising), no personal data that could identify the user is collected. Only statistical data related to app interactions is gathered: crashes, freezes, launched activities, completed activities, obtained results (anonymous), etc.

These insights help analyze interactions, diagnose issues, and improve the app to better meet users' expectations.

The collected data is not shared with third parties outside of the services provided by Google (Google Analytics, Firebase, Google AdMob).

Some information necessary for the app’s functionality and activity progression (completed exercises, validated exercises, result tracking, etc.) is stored locally. These can be deleted simply by clearing the app's data.

Requested Permissions

The app requires certain permissions to function properly:

  • Vibration: Used to provide haptic feedback during specific interactions.
  • Internet Access: Necessary to load online content and display ads.
  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads.

Children

Numeration Primary is suitable for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove that information from our systems.

Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes to this policy will be posted here.

Contact
