Privacy Policy - Math Exercises

Introduction

Welcome to Math Exercises. Your privacy is important. This privacy policy describes the information collected from your device. The application does not require an internet connection to function, and no personal information identifying the user is collected.

Data Collection

Apart from the advertising ID (which may be used to display targeted ads in accordance with applicable regulations), no personal data that could identify the user is collected. Only statistical data related to app interactions is gathered: crashes, freezes, launched activities, completed activities, obtained results (anonymous), etc.

This information is useful to analyze interactions, diagnose issues, and improve the app to better match users’ expectations.

The collected data is not shared with third parties. However, it is collected using analytics tools provided by Google (Google Analytics, Firebase, Google AdMob).

Some information required for the app to function and for tracking progress (completed exercises, validated tasks, result history, etc.) is stored locally. It can be deleted simply by clearing the app’s data.

Requested Permissions

The app requires certain permissions to function properly:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to send reminders and encourage regular app usage.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): to schedule reminders precisely.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): used to provide haptic feedback during certain interactions.
  • Internet access (INTERNET): required to load online content and display ads.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check connection availability before accessing the internet.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by Google AdMob to serve non-personalized ads.

Children

Math Exercises is suitable for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated at any time. Any changes will be published here.
